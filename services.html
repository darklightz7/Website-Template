OUR IN-SHOP SERVICES & SPECIALIZED APPLICATIONS

Engineered high-pressure polyurea spray-on bedliners, UV-resistant armor, rocker panels, and commercial fleet packages applied inside our Euless, TX shop bays.

Standard Spray-On Truck Bedliners

Our flagship high-pressure hot polyurea spray-on bedliners form a permanent airtight, watertight seal directly bonded to your truck bed substrate. Eliminates rust pockets, absorbs extreme impact, and provides high-traction non-skid cargo grip.

  • 10-Year Transferable Warranty Included
  • High-Traction Non-Skid Textured Finish
  • Rapid Cure: Light Duty in 4-6 Hours, Full Cargo in 24 Hours
  • Under-Rail and Over-the-Rail Applications

UV Premium Protective Topcoats

Formulated specifically for the intense Texas sun. Our aliphatic UV topcoat chemically bonds with the polyurea liner to permanently prevent dulling, chalking, and gray fading, maintaining a factory-fresh satin sheen for the life of your truck.

  • Aliphatic UV Chemical Inhibitors
  • Prevents Solar Chalking and Graying
  • Enhanced Chemical & Fuel Spill Resistance
  • Satin Gloss or Deep Jet-Black Retention

Rocker Panels & Wheel Wells

Highway gravel, oversized tire fling, and off-road rocks destroy lower cab paint. Our rocker panel and wheel well coating provides a thick, resilient impact shield along door sills, cab corners, and wheel wells with precision wire-trimmed cutlines.

  • Extreme Rock Chip & Debris Deflection
  • Carbon-Filament Wire Trimming for Clean Lines
  • Eliminates Fender Well Rusting & Salt Corrosion
  • Available in Textured Black or Color Tint
