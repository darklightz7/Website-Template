Our In-Shop Mission

At Blackhawk Protective Coatings, our shop was engineered specifically for vehicle bedliner and automotive protective applications. We believe that true, lifetime-grade truck protection cannot be achieved in a driveway or mobile rig. It requires a closed, clean, climate-stabilized spray booth, industrial air handling, and high-pressure heating reactors.

  • Dedicated 6,000+ sq. ft. Drive-In Facility in Euless, TX
  • Industrial Down-Draft Ventilation & Zero Overspray Guarantee
  • Plural-Component High-Pressure Hot Spray (160°F+, 2,500 PSI)
  • 10-Year Transferable Warranty on Every Truck Bed

THE IN-SHOP EQUIPMENT ADVANTAGE

Why true polyurea spray-in liners require an in-shop industrial proportioning setup.

⚡

High-Pressure Heated Reactor

Our plural-component proportioners pre-heat isocyanate and resin components to 160°F+ at 2,500+ PSI. This triggers instant molecular cross-linking upon striking the bed.

INSTANT MOLECULAR BOND
🎯

Wire-Trim Edge Precision

We use carbon-filament cutting tape to achieve razor-sharp, factory-grade transition lines along bed rails, tailgates, and wheel tubs without razor blades ever touching your factory paint.

ZERO PAINT SCORING
🛡️

Full Vehicle Static Masking

Your vehicle is completely enclosed in electrostatically-charged poly sheeting inside our spray bay. Only the intended application zone is exposed, ensuring zero overspray.

100% OVERSPRAY PROTECTION
💎

Mechanical Substrate Profiling

We thoroughly degrease and mechanically abrade the factory clear coat using coarse cup wheels to create an aggressive mechanical anchor profile that never bubbles or peels.

PERMANENT ADHESION
