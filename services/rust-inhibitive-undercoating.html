Chassis & Frame Protection

RUST-INHIBITIVE CHASSIS UNDERCOATING

Comprehensive undercarriage and frame rail elastomeric seal applied on our shop vehicle lifts to prevent corrosion, salt pitting, and road noise.

Heavy-Duty Undercarriage Preservation

Road de-icers, highway salts, gravel spray, and persistent humidity wreak havoc on truck frame rails, crossmembers, floor pans, and suspension brackets. At Blackhawk Protective Coatings, your vehicle is raised on our heavy-duty shop lifts for an exhaustive underbody prep. We steam-clean contaminants, mask off exhaust and moving components, and apply a resilient elastomeric barrier that blocks rust permanently.

  • Self-Healing Elastomeric Shield Resists Gravel Impacts
  • Full Frame Rails, Crossmembers, Floor Pans & Inner Wheel Houses
  • Significantly Dampens Highway Road Noise & Frame Resonance
  • Applied In-Shop with Precision Component Masking
