Lower Body & Undercarriage Defense

ROCKER PANELS & WHEEL WELLS ARMOR

Shield lower cab sheet metal, door sills, cab corners, and wheel tubs against highway gravel blast, tire sling, and off-road rock impacts.

Heavy-Duty Impact Protection for Vulnerable Body Lines

The lower 6 to 10 inches of your truck's cab and bed corners absorb continuous bombardment from stones thrown by your tires. Once factory clear coat chips, moisture and road salts quickly induce rocker panel rust that compromises structural pinch welds. Our in-shop polyurea rocker panel application provides an impenetrable elastomeric barrier with razor-clean lines.

  • Complete Rock Chip Deflection Along Sills & Cab Corners
  • Precision Carbon-Filament Wire-Trim Lines (No Razor Blades)
  • Inner Wheel Well Noise Reduction & Mud Sling Defense
  • Available in Textured Matte Black or Color Match
