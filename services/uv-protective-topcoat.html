UV PREMIUM PROTECTIVE TOPCOATS

Engineered aliphatic technology that permanently shields your bedliner against harsh North Texas solar radiation, chalking, and discoloration.

Why Texas Trucks Need Aliphatic UV Protection

Standard aromatic polyurea coatings are renowned for their incredible tensile strength, but high solar ultraviolet radiation will naturally break down aromatic chemical bonds over time, causing the coating to turn a dull, powdery gray. Our UV Premium Protective Topcoat utilizes advanced aliphatic chemistry that is chemically impervious to UV photolytic degradation.

  • 100% Aliphatic UV Photostable Molecular Backbone
  • Eliminates Solar Oxidation, Chalking & Graying
  • Resists Diesel Fuel, Hydraulic Fluids & Battery Acid
  • Deep Satin Sheen That Cleans Effortlessly
