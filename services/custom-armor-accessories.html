CUSTOM TRUCK ARMOR & ACCESSORIES

Industrial polyurea encapsulation for steel bumpers, headache racks, toolboxes, side steps, and off-road armor that completely outperforms powder coating.

The Superior Alternative to Powder Coating

Traditional powder coating is thin, brittle, and notoriously vulnerable to rock chips. Once a rock chips powder coat, water enters beneath the surface, creating hidden rust pockets that cause the paint to flake off in sheets. Our high-pressure hot polyurea encapsulates your steel accessories in a flexible, 80-mil thick impact shield that never chips, peels, or allows rust to creep.

  • Outlasts Powder Coating by 5x Against Rock & Trail Scrapes
  • Full Encapsulation of Aftermarket Steel Bumpers & Grille Guards
  • Headache Racks, Side Steps, Rock Sliders & Toolboxes
  • Available in Textured Matte Black, Gunmetal, or Desert Tan
