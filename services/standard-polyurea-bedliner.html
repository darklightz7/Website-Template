In-Shop Spray-On Bedliner

STANDARD POLYUREA SPRAY-ON BEDLINERS

Applied hot at 160°F+ under 2,500+ PSI pressure in our Euless shop bays for an unbreakable, watertight molecular bond.

Engineered High-Pressure Performance

Our standard polyurea spray-on truck bedliner represents the pinnacle of modern elastomeric coating science. Using industrial Graco Reactor proportioners, we heat isocyanate and amine resin components to over 160°F and mix them at the spray gun tip at 2,500 PSI. The result is instant chemical polymerization that forms a seamless, 125-mil thick protective armor permanently bonded to your truck bed.

  • 2,800+ PSI Tensile Strength & 300% Elongation
  • 100% Watertight Seal Eliminates Trapped Moisture & Rust
  • Tactile Non-Skid Texture Prevents Cargo Shift
  • 10-Year Nationwide Transferable Adhesion Warranty

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & PROCESS

Every step of our in-shop bedliner application is engineered for lifetime longevity.

🔬

Substrate Profiling

We mechanically score the factory clear coat using coarse cup wheels to create a 2 to 3 mil anchor profile. We never rely on acid washes or chemical scuff pads alone.

MECHANICAL ADHESION
⚡

Wire-Trim Edge Precision

Carbon-filament cutting tape is pre-applied along bed rails, tailgate jams, and tie-down points. When pulled, it creates clean factory cutlines without razor blades.

ZERO PAINT DAMAGE
⏱️

Instant Chemical Cure

Tack-free within 5 seconds of spraying. Ready for driving immediately upon pickup, light cargo in 4-6 hours, and full heavy-duty freight within 24 hours.

RAPID TURNAROUND
🛡️

Rail Coverage Options

Choose between Under-the-Rail (for trucks with factory plastic rail protectors) or Over-the-Rail (maximum protection against sliding ladders and toolboxes).
