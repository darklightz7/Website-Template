DRIVE-IN HIGHWAY DIRECTIONS TO EULESS FACILITY

Located in the heart of the DFW Mid-Cities, we are conveniently reachable from anywhere in North Texas.

🚗

From Dallas & East DFW

Take I-30 W or Hwy 183 W (Airport Freeway) past Irving directly into Euless. Exit Industrial Blvd, proceed north 0.4 miles. Our facility is on your right.

Approx. 20-25 Minutes
🚗

From Fort Worth & West Tarrant

Take I-30 E or Hwy 121 N / Airport Freeway E towards DFW Airport. Take the Industrial Blvd exit, turn left under the freeway, and proceed north to 4820 Industrial Blvd.

Approx. 18-22 Minutes
🚗

From Plano, Frisco & McKinney

Take the Sam Rayburn Tollway (Hwy 121 S) past Grapevine directly into Euless, or take President George Bush Turnpike (PGBT) to Hwy 183 W. Exit Industrial Blvd.

Approx. 25-30 Minutes
🚗

From Arlington & South DFW

Take Hwy 360 N directly into Euless/Grapevine. Merge onto Hwy 183 W (Airport Freeway) toward Fort Worth, exit Industrial Blvd and head north.

Approx. 12-15 Minutes
